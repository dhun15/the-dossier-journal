ABOUT THE JOURNAL

Beyond the
day's headlines.

The Dossier Journal is an independent publication devoted to serious inquiry and clear argument.

It brings together perspectives on political thought, historical change, public policy and international affairs.

We believe that understanding the present requires curiosity about the past, attention to evidence and a willingness to question easy answers.
